Scoring System

Longer words.
Bigger points.

Every word scores letter points (like Scrabble) amplified by a length multiplier. A 7-letter word is worth ~5× more than a 4-letter one. Rare letters — J, Q, X, Z — pay dividends.

In timed modes, each word you find also adds seconds back to the clock. Longer words add more — a 9-letter word buys you a full minute.
